Privacy

Privacy and cookies

What this website collects about you, and how to turn it off.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and shows people look at, so we can improve the site. It records things like the pages you view, the site that sent you here, your device and browser type, your rough location (city or country), and clicks on ticket links. It does not give us your name or contact details.

We have switched off Google's advertising features, so this data is not used for ads or to build an advertising profile of you.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets two cookies: _ga and _ga_C2RBGG9435. They let it tell new visits from returning ones and last up to two years. UK law allows analytics cookies used only to improve a website without asking first, as long as you can easily opt out.

Opting out

Use (also in the footer of every page) and choose Opt out. We delete the analytics cookies and stop measuring your visits on this browser. We remember that choice in your browser's local storage, so clearing your browsing data resets it.

Tickets and the mailing list

Tickets are booked on Eventbrite and the mailing list runs on beehiiv. When you use them, their own privacy policies apply.

Contact

For questions about your data, email londoncomedygroup@gmail.com or message London Comedy Group on Instagram or Facebook. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
